How to review and what the suggestions mean

Start with runs containing useful LEFT/RIGHT examples. Watch the retained section, then inspect the end and any warnings. Adjust the cuts to exclude the failed decision, not merely the death animation. Short and medium runs can be useful too.

Suggestions remove the first 2.15 seconds and last 2.5 seconds and may stop before a capture gap. These are unverified starting points; they are not the individually reviewed cuts from the small pilot. An opening can reappear later after a restart. In that case skip the run or flag it rather than assuming later auxiliary history is correct.

Recorded timestamps are mapped to video frames. Do not use the video player's clock as the exact training timestamp. Only windows with valid four-frame histories and at least one physical action can be kept. This checks data consistency, not expert quality.

Use “Add current interval” for separated clean sections. The displayed window is always included along with saved additional intervals. Export your decisions when finished and give the JSON back to the assistant. No raw files are changed and no training starts from this page.
